KAUSHAL KUMAR — FREE DIWALI EMAIL SURPRISE

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- diwali-card.gif: animated card to insert directly into an email (email apps that support GIF animation will play it).
- index.html: interactive surprise page with a gift button and fireworks.

PUBLISH THE INTERACTIVE PAGE FREE
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in/create a free account.
3. Drag the extracted "site" folder onto the Netlify Drop page.
4. Copy the public website address Netlify creates (it ends in netlify.app).

SEND THE EMAIL
1. Open Gmail and click Compose.
2. Subject: A little Diwali surprise for you! 🪔
3. Insert the animated GIF: click Insert photo and upload diwali-card.gif.
4. Add this line below it: "Your special surprise is waiting — click here: YOUR_NETLIFY_LINK"
5. Replace YOUR_NETLIFY_LINK with your published Netlify address, then send.

IMPORTANT
- The GIF can animate in the email itself, depending on the recipient's email app/settings.
- The interactive gift-opening fireworks happen after the recipient clicks the website link. Email apps block scripts/React inside email for security, so the full interactive experience cannot run directly inside Gmail.
- The page uses plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript (no React setup required), so it is simpler and loads without external libraries.
